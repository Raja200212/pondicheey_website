We The Leaders - Reels For Real Change 2026
Rules & Regulations

1. Eligibility & Participation
- Eligibility: Open to participants from Tamil Nadu and Puducherry.
- Participation: Solo or team entries are allowed, with a maximum of 4 members per team.
- Registration: Participation and registration are completely free.
- Certificate: Eligible participants will receive a verified participation certificate.

2. Reel Requirements
- Duration: Each reel must be between 1 and 3 minutes.
- Format: Vertical 9:16 format only.
- Resolution: Minimum 1080p.
- Language: Tamil, English and other local languages are allowed.
- Subtitles: English subtitles are compulsory.
- Theme: The reel must clearly address one of the official impact themes.

3. Official Impact Themes
- 01 Youth Leadership
- 02 Social Awareness
- 03 Civic Pride
- 04 Environmental Sustainability
- 05 Gender Equality
- 06 Other officially announced impact themes

4. Instagram Submission Process
- Complete the official registration form with the required details.
- Post the completed reel on your public Instagram profile.
- Send a Collab Request to @wtl_leadingthechange.
- Submit the published Instagram Reel link through the registration form.
- Save the Registration ID received after successful submission.

5. Originality & Content Standards
- Original Work: Submitted reels must be original content created by the participant/team.
- Rights & Permissions: Participants are responsible for permissions relating to third-party material.
- Responsible Content: Content must not contain inappropriate material.
- Authenticity: Content should be respectful, responsible and relevant to the chosen theme.

6. Submission & Deadline
- Last Date: All entries must be submitted on or before 25 October 2026.
- Public Profile: The Instagram profile used for submission must remain public for verification.
- Late Entries: Entries submitted after the deadline may not be considered.

7. Evaluation & Recognition
- Theme Relevance: Clarity and connection to the selected impact theme.
- Creativity: Originality of concept, storytelling and presentation.
- Social Impact: Strength of the message and potential to inspire positive change.
- Execution: Overall quality, editing, visuals, clarity and effective use of the reel format.

8. Disqualification
- Entries that fail to follow the official requirements may be rejected.
- Plagiarised, duplicate or unauthorised content may lead to disqualification.
- Prohibited or inappropriate content will not be considered.
- False or misleading registration information may invalidate an entry.
